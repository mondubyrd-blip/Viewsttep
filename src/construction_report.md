# Gambrel Shed Construction Report

## Build Specifications
- **Shed footprint:** 16 ft wide × 20 ft long
- **Wall height:** 8 ft
- **Roof:** Gambrel barn roof
- **Foundation:** 15 pier layout (3x5 grid)
- **Beams:** Triple-ply 2x10 pressure-treated
- **Floor:** 2x8 PT joists @ 16" O.C.
- **Subfloor:** 3/4" T&G OSB

## Construction Progress Log
*   **PHASE 0:** Site Location defined. Backyard orientation: North-oriented.
*   **PHASE 1:** Foundation Layout marked with batter boards.
*   **PHASE 2:** 15 Pier points excavated and cast in Sonotubes.
*   **PHASE 3:** Main support beams installed.
*   **PHASE 4:** Floor framing completed with rim joists and blocking.
*   **PHASE 5:** OSB subfloor glued and screwed.
*   **PHASE 6:** Walls raised (Side walls first, then framed Front/Rear).
*   **PHASE 7:** Loft joists and deck installed.
*   **PHASE 8:** Gambrel rafters and gussets installed.
*   **PHASE 9:** OSB wall and roof sheathing applied.
*   **PHASE 10:** Weather wrap and metal roofing installed.
*   **PHASE 11:** Interior systems (Insulation, Electrical, Fish Room zones).
*   **PHASE 12:** Final walkthrough and orbital view.
